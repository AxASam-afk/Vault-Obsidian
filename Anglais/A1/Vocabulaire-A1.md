## 📚 Exercices de Révision

### Exercice 1 : Catégorisation
Classez ces mots : *apple, brother, red, kitchen, happy, bus, Monday, big, read, under*

### Exercice 2 : Paires (opposés)
big ↔ \_\_\_\_\_ | hot ↔ \_\_\_\_\_ | day ↔ \_\_\_\_\_ | happy ↔ \_\_\_\_\_ | expensive ↔ \_\_\_\_\_

### Exercice 3 : Mots cachés
Trouvez 10 mots A1 dans : *THESCHOOLBAGISONTHECHAIR*

### Exercice 4 : Phrases à trous
1. I \_\_\_\_\_ (eat) breakfast at 7am.
2. My \_\_\_\_\_ (mother) is a teacher.
3. The \_\_\_\_\_ (book) is on the table.
4. It's \_\_\_\_\_ (rain) today.
