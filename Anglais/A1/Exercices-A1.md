## 📋 Test de Positionnement A1

### Section 1 : Grammaire (20 points)

**Complétez avec *am / is / are* :**
1. My name \_\_\_\_\_ Thomas.
2. We \_\_\_\_\_ students.
3. She \_\_\_\_\_ from London.
4. They \_\_\_\_\_ happy.
5. It \_\_\_\_\_ a beautiful day.

**Mettez au négatif :**
6. I am French. → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
7. He has got a car. → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
8. They are teachers. → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
9. We have got a dog. → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
10. She is hungry. → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

**Formez les questions :**
11. you / like / pizza → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
12. he / speak / English → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
13. they / live / in Paris → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
14. she / have got / a brother → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
15. it / rain / often → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

**Present Simple - Conjuguez :**
16. I usually \_\_\_\_\_ (get up) at 7am.
17. She \_\_\_\_\_ (not / like) coffee.
18. \_\_\_\_\_ they \_\_\_\_\_ (play) football?
19. My brother \_\_\_\_\_ (watch) TV every evening.
20. We \_\_\_\_\_ (not / work) on Sundays.

### Section 2 : Vocabulaire (20 points)

**Traduisez en anglais :**
1. Bonjour, comment allez-vous ? → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
2. J'ai 25 ans. → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
3. C'est mon frère. → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
4. La cuisine est grande. → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
5. Je mange une pomme. → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
6. Combien ça coûte ? → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
7. Où est la gare ? → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
8. Je vais au cinéma demain. → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
9. Il fait beau aujourd'hui. → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
10. Au revoir ! → \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

**Complétez avec le bon mot :**
11. My \_\_\_\_\_ (mother/father) is a woman.
12. I \_\_\_\_\_ (have/has) got blue eyes.
13. The book is \_\_\_\_\_ (on/under) the table.
14. \_\_\_\_\_ (What/Where) is your name?
15. \_\_\_\_\_ (How much/How many) apples?

**Famille - Choisissez le bon terme :**
16. My mother's brother = \_\_\_\_\_ (uncle/cousin)
17. My father's sister = \_\_\_\_\_ (aunt/niece)
18. My uncle's daughter = \_\_\_\_\_ (cousin/niece)
19. My brother's son = \_\_\_\_\_ (nephew/cousin)
20. My sister's daughter = \_\_\_\_\_ (niece/nephew)

### Section 3 : Compréhension Écrite (10 points)

**Lisez le texte et répondez :**

> *Hello! My name is Sarah. I'm 22 years old and I'm from Manchester, in England. I'm a student at university. I study French and Spanish. I've got a small family: my parents and one brother. His name is David and he's 19. He's a student too. We live in a flat near the city centre. My favourite colour is blue and I like pizza. In my free time, I read books and listen to music. I don't like sport very much.*

**Vrai ou Faux ?**
1. Sarah is 22 years old. □ True □ False
2. She is from London. □ True □ False
3. She studies French and German. □ True □ False
4. She has got one brother. □ True □ False
5. Her brother's name is David. □ True □ False
6. They live in a house. □ True □ False
7. Her favourite colour is red. □ True □ False
8. She likes reading. □ True □ False
9. She likes sport. □ True □ False
10. David is older than Sarah. □ True □ False

## 🎯 Exercices Ciblés par Thème

### Thème 1 : Être & Avoir (to be / have got)
Transformez les phrases :
1. I am a student. → Négatif / Question / *He*
2. They have got a car. → Négatif / Question / *She*
3. It is cold today. → Négatif / Question / *We*

### Thème 2 : Present Simple
Conjuguez aux 3 formes (✓ / ✗ / ?) :
- *work* (I / he / they)
- *study* (she / we / you)
- *live* (it / I / they)
- *have* (he / we / she) → *has / have / has*

### Thème 3 : Prépositions de Lieu
Complétez : *The cat is \_\_\_\_\_ the box.* (in/on/under/next to/behind/in front of)

### Thème 4 : There is / There are
1. \_\_\_\_\_ a book on the table.
2. \_\_\_\_\_ three chairs in the room.
3. \_\_\_\_\_ not a pen in my bag.
4. \_\_\_\_\_ any apples?
5. How many students \_\_\_\_\_ in the class?

### Thème 5 : Can / Can't (Capacité/Permission)
Complétez :
1. I \_\_\_\_\_ swim. (savoir nager)
2. \_\_\_\_\_ I open the window? (permission)
3. Birds \_\_\_\_\_ fly. (capacité)
4. You \_\_\_\_\_ park here. (interdiction - *can't*)
5. \_\_\_\_\_ you speak Spanish? (capacité)

## 🎮 Jeux de Révision

### 1. **Memory Cards** (Cartes mémoire)
Créez des paires : *Anglais ↔ Français* ou *Image ↔ Mot*

### 2. **Bingo A1**
Grille 4x4 avec vocabulaire : *apple, book, cat, dog, house, car, tree, sun, rain, snow, happy, sad, big, small, hot, cold*

### 3. **Pictionary** (Dessin)
Dessinez : *kitchen, brother, swimming, umbrella, sandwich, evening*

### 4. **Chain Story** (Histoire en chaîne)
Chaque personne ajoute une phrase au *Present Simple* :
*\"I wake up at 7.\" → \"I have breakfast.\" → \"I go to school.\" → ...*

### 5. **20 Questions**
Devinez un mot/personnage en posant des questions *Yes/No* :
*\"Is it a person?\" \"Is it a man?\" \"Does he live in London?\"...*
