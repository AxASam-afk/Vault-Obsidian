### Dizaines (30-90)

| Chiffre | Anglais | Note |
|---------|---------|------|
| 30 | **thirty** | /zèrti/ |
| 40 | **forty** | *pas four-ty* ! |
| 50 | **fifty** | /fifti/ |
| 60 | **sixty** | /siksti/ |
| 70 | **seventy** | /sèvènti/ |
| 80 | **eighty** | /eïti/ |
| 90 | **ninety** | /naïnti/ |

### Composition (21-99)

> **Règle** : Dizaine + *hyphen* + Unité
> - 21 = **twenty-one**
> - 45 = **forty-five**
> - 99 = **ninety-nine**

### Grands nombres

| Nombre | Anglais |
|--------|---------|
| 100 | **one hundred** / **a hundred** |
| 101 | **one hundred and one** |
| 1,000 | **one thousand** / **a thousand** |
| 1,000,000 | **one million** |

> 🇬🇧 **UK** : *and* entre centaines et dizaines (101 = one hundred **and** one)  
> 🇺🇸 **US** : souvent sans *and* (101 = one hundred one)

## 📅 Jours de la Semaine (Days of the Week)

| Jour | Anglais | Abréviation | Prononciation |
|------|---------|-------------|---------------|
| Lundi | **Monday** | Mon | **Mandeï** |
| Mardi | **Tuesday** | Tue | **Tiouzdeï** |
| Mercredi | **Wednesday** | Wed | **Wènzdeï** |
| Jeudi | **Thursday** | Thu | **Zeuzdeï** |
| Vendredi | **Friday** | Fri | **Fraïdeï** |
| Samedi | **Saturday** | Sat | **Sateudeï** |
| Dimanche | **Sunday** | Sun | **Sandeï** |

> ⚠️ **Majuscule obligatoire** en anglais pour les jours !

### Expressions utiles

- **What day is it today?** → *Today is Monday.*
- **What day is it tomorrow?** → *Tomorrow is Tuesday.*
- **the weekend** = *Saturday and Sunday*
- **on Monday** (le lundi), **on Mondays** (le lundi / tous les lundis)

## 📅 Donner la Date

### Format UK (Jour/Mois/Année)
> **the 1st of January 2024** → *the first of January twenty twenty-four*

### Format US (Mois/Jour/Année)
> **January 1st, 2024** → *January first, twenty twenty-four*

### Nombres ordinaux (pour les dates)

| Chiffre | Ordinal | Prononciation |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| 1 | **1st** (first) | fèrst |
| 2 | **2nd** (second) | sèconde |
| 3 | **3rd** (third) | zerde |
| 4-20 | **th** (fourth, fifth...) | ème |
| 21 | **21st** (twenty-first) | twènte-fèrst |
| 22 | **22nd** (twenty-second) | twènte-sèconde |
| 23 | **23rd** (twenty-third) | twènte-zerde |

### Prépositions de temps

| Préposition | Utilisation | Exemple |
|-------------|-------------|---------|
| **on** | Jour précis | *on Monday, on the 1st of May* |
| **in** | Mois, année, saison | *in January, in 2024, in summer* |
| **at** | Heure précise | *at 5 o'clock, at noon* |
